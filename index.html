RETAIL REALITIES

The Challenges Modern Retailers Face

From neighborhood grocery stores and fashion boutiques to electronics showrooms and multi-location retail chains, checkout errors erode profitability.

CHALLENGE 01

Cash Drawer Shortages

Manual change calculations, untracked cash movements, and inconsistent end-of-day reconciliation make it difficult to identify discrepancies and maintain accurate cash balances.

CHALLENGE 02

Fake UPI Screenshots

Unverified payment screenshots and pending transactions can result in unpaid bills being marked as completed. Retailers need payment confirmation linked to the exact invoice amount.

CHALLENGE 03

Inventory Inaccuracy

Disconnected sales and stock records lead to phantom stockouts, overselling, misplaced inventory, and unnecessary overstocking.

CHALLENGE 04

Internet Dependency

Cloud-dependent billing systems can interrupt checkout when connectivity fails, resulting in long queues, abandoned purchases, and manual reconciliation work.

Built for the high-velocity retail counter

Everything your business needs to bill faster, reduce errors, and maintain complete operational oversight.

High-Speed Countertop Billing

Bill customers quickly with barcode scanning and keyboard shortcuts (F1 Search, F2 Customer, F4 Discount, F8 Hold cart, F9 Cash, F10 UPI). Visual category tiles and quick-access products.

Smart Cash Tender and Change Management

For a bill of ₹847: Cash received ₹1,000, Change to return ₹153, Net cash added to drawer ₹847. Automatic change calculation, quick cash tender, under-tender handling, and split payments.

Dynamic UPI Payment Verification

Invoice-linked dynamic UPI QR codes. Confirm payment status through verified provider webhooks before marking bills paid. Prevent fake screenshots and duplicate notifications.

Auditable Stock Ledger & Inventory Management

Record stock movements across sales, returns, supplier goods receipts (GRN), purchase adjustments, write-offs, and inter-store transfers. Low-stock alerts and purchase orders.

Shift Management and Cash Reconciliation

Record opening cash floats by denomination, track authorized pay-ins/pay-outs and safe drops, enforce blind cash counts, and lock End-of-Day Z reports.

Customer Management, Khata and Loyalty

Digital Khata store credit, credit limits, automated WhatsApp and SMS balance reminders, and configurable loyalty reward points (1 point per ₹100 spent) redeemable at checkout.
