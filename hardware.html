SECTION 10 • DEVICE ECOSYSTEM

Supported Device Categories

COMPUTERS

Windows, Mac & Laptops

Windows laptops and desktop computers, compatible iPads, and Mac computers. Continue billing offline with local data storage.

TABLETS

Android Tablets & Terminals

Compatible Android tablets, touchscreen stands, and dedicated commercial retail Android POS terminals.

SCANNERS

1D & 2D Barcode Scanners

USB and Bluetooth barcode scanners, wireless handheld scanners, and supported 1D and 2D barcode readers for sub-0.4s scan-to-cart checkout.

PRINTERS

ESC/POS Thermal Printers

Compatible 58 mm and 80 mm ESC/POS thermal printers. Potentially supported printer families include Epson TM-T82, TVS RP-3200, Star Micronics, NGX, and compatible Bluetooth thermal printers.

DRAWERS

Cash Drawer Support

Compatible cash drawers triggered automatically through supported printer kick interfaces and kick commands upon completing cash sales.

GUIDANCE

Hardware Guides

Step-by-step guidance for connecting barcode scanners, pairing Bluetooth thermal printers, configuring receipt widths and print settings, connecting cash drawers, and testing hardware before live checkout.
