SECTION 3 • CASH TENDER & CHANGE

Every Rupee Accounted For

Cashiers enter the amount received, view the exact change due, and record the transaction in the shift ledger automatically.

AUTOMATIC CHANGE

Automatic Change Calculation

For a bill of ₹847:
• Total amount due: ₹847
• Cash received: ₹1,000
• Change to return: ₹153
• Net cash added to the drawer: ₹847
The system calculates change instantly and records the tendered amount, change returned, and resulting drawer balance.

QUICK TENDER

Quick Cash Tender

Choose a suggested cash amount or enter the exact amount received. The system calculates the change automatically with zero mental calculation strain.

SPLIT PAYMENTS

Split Payments & Under-Tender

When a customer pays less than the bill, view the remaining balance. Accept combinations in a single transaction:
• Cash: ₹500
• UPI: ₹300
• Store credit: ₹47
• Total bill: ₹847
The receipt records the payment breakdown for transparent reconciliation.

AUDIT TRAIL

Cash Drawer Audit Trail

Record cash received, change returned, cash deposits, withdrawals, and other authorized movements. Each movement contributes to the expected drawer balance.

DENOMINATION TRACKING

Shift Denomination Tracking

Record physical currency by denomination during opening and closing counts. Compare the actual cash counted against the expected balance to identify shortages or overages.

RECONCILIATION

Shift Cash Reconciliation

Start every shift with opening floats by denomination. Enforce blind closing counts and lock tamper-proof End-of-Day Z reports.

SECTION 4 • DIGITAL PAYMENTS

Dynamic UPI Payment Verification

Verify Payments. Reduce Fraud. Keep Checkout Moving. CrossTech POS connects digital payment verification with individual invoices rather than relying solely on screenshots or customer confirmation.

DYNAMIC QR

Invoice-Linked Dynamic UPI QR

Generate a payment QR code associated with the exact bill amount and invoice. Customers scan the code using a supported UPI application to initiate payment.

WEBHOOK STATUS

Payment Status Verification

Use supported payment-provider integrations to confirm payment status through verified webhooks or provider status checks. A bill should only be marked as paid digitally after payment has been verified by the relevant provider.

STATUS HANDLING

Pending and Failed Payments

Handle pending, failed, cancelled, and expired payment attempts explicitly. Configure QR expiry and retry behavior to prevent incomplete payments from being treated as successful sales.

RECONCILIATION

Payment Reconciliation

Match verified payment records with sales invoices to make it easier to identify missing payments, duplicate notifications, and reconciliation discrepancies.

INTEGRATION NOTE

Provider Dependency Note

Actual payment verification, dynamic QR generation, and webhook functionality depend on integration with a supported payment provider. An offline counter cannot independently verify a new online UPI payment while disconnected.

ZERO COMMISSION

Direct Provider Settlement

No additional CrossTech software fee per transaction. Funds settle directly into your bank account through your merchant UPI provider.
