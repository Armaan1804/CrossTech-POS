SECTION 7 • KHATA & REWARDS ENGINE

Digital Khata, Credit Limits, and Loyalty Points

STORE CREDIT

Customer Ledger (Khata)

Track customer balances, credit purchases, repayments, and outstanding dues in a dedicated digital ledger. Every credit transaction is tied to an itemized invoice number.

RISK CONTROLS

Credit Limit Controls

Set credit limits per customer. The checkout screen notifies the cashier when an order exceeds the customer's credit limit and can require manager override or block the sale.

REPAYMENTS

Payment Collection

Record partial or full repayments against outstanding customer balances. Print or send a payment receipt and instantly update the customer's remaining balance in real time.

REMINDERS

Automated Payment Reminders

Send friendly payment reminder messages via supported WhatsApp or SMS integrations, including the current outstanding balance and integrated UPI payment link.

LOYALTY PROGRAM

Customer Loyalty Points

Award loyalty points based on purchase amount. Configure redemption rules, minimum points thresholds, and apply loyalty points directly as a checkout discount.

SHOPPER INSIGHTS

Customer Purchase History

View past invoices, frequently bought items, purchase patterns, and total lifetime spend to provide personalized service and recommend complementary products at checkout.
