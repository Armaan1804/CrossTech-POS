BARCODE SCANNING

High-Speed Barcode Scanning

Scan products using compatible USB, Bluetooth, wireless, or camera-based barcode scanners. Supported barcode formats include EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 128, and QR codes. Products are added to the active cart with immediate visual and audio feedback.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

Keyboard-Driven Checkout

Complete checkout without constantly reaching for a mouse: F1 Search catalog, F2 Select customer, F4 Apply discount, F8 Hold cart, F9 Cash checkout, F10 UPI checkout, ESC Void or cancel current operation.

VISUAL ACCESS

Visual Categories & Quick Access

Access frequently sold products through visual category tiles and configurable quick-access buttons. Categories can include beverages, snacks, groceries, dairy, apparel, and other store-specific products.

QUEUE MANAGEMENT

Hold and Recall Orders

Temporarily park an unfinished cart when a customer needs more time or steps away from the counter. Continue billing the next customer and recall the earlier cart when ready.

FLEXIBLE TENDER

Flexible Multi-Tender Checkout

Support multiple payment methods within one bill, including cash, UPI, cards, and store credit where enabled.

RECEIPTS

Thermal & Digital Receipts

Print receipts on compatible 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers with store branding, item details, applicable GST breakdowns, and discounts. Send digital receipts via supported WhatsApp or SMS integrations.
