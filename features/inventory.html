Every Sale. Every Stock Movement. Fully Accounted For.

Preserve the history of stock changes instead of allowing unexplained inventory edits.

STOCK LEDGER

Auditable Inventory Ledger

Record inventory movements with quantities, timestamps, transaction references, and authorized reasons. Supported movement types include: Sales & returns, Supplier goods receipts, Purchase adjustments, Damaged or written-off goods, Inter-store transfers, Authorized stock corrections.

GOODS RECEIPT

Supplier Goods Receipt (GRN)

Record incoming stock against supplier purchases, update inventory, track purchase costs, and generate barcode labels where supported.

REPLENISHMENT

Low-Stock Alerts

Set minimum stock levels for individual products. Identify items that need replenishment and prepare purchase orders for registered suppliers before shelves empty.

PROCUREMENT

Supplier Purchase Orders

Create and manage purchase orders, track incoming goods, and maintain supplier records to support more organized purchasing.

MULTI-STORE

Multi-Store Stock Transfers

Move inventory between outlets or warehouses through a documented transfer workflow:
1. Dispatch the stock.
2. Track the transfer while in transit.
3. Receive and verify the delivered quantities.

CATALOG & COSTING

Product Barcodes & Inventory Costing

Maintain product names, SKUs, barcodes, categories, prices, tax information, and stock quantities. Generate compatible barcode labels. Support inventory costing methods such as FIFO or weighted average, subject to configured accounting rules.
